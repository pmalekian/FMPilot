# FM Pilot

Frankie Malekian's personal fork of [AdventurePilot](https://github.com/AdventurePilotDev/AdventurePilot) (`dev`), for a
2022 Rivian R1S (Gen 1) with a comma 4. Branches: `fm-dev-set` (current development) and `fm-dev-jet` (`fm-dev-set`
plus Jetlink); `fm-dev-max` is the older everyday branch. Everything AdventurePilot does is kept; FM Pilot adds the
features below. Every FM Pilot commit starts with "FM Pilot".

## Features

| Accel Boost, one of four (exclusive, never stacked): **Off**; **Gas** (Gas Override Boost: after the driver presses the gas while the model holds the car below what the lead and set speed allow, adds up to +0.2 m/s² to the model's acceleration for that engagement); **Predictive** (sunnypilot's predictive boost: adds acceleration whenever the model predicts no reason to slow, up to +0.4 / 0.25 / 0.1 m/s² for aggressive / standard / relaxed, fading out in tight curves and when the model wants to stop); **Set Speed** (EVPilot's Set Speed Assist: above 20 mph, and down to 10 mph for 3 s after a lead it braked for leaves, raises the model's acceleration toward the cruise ramp, which tapers to 0 at the set speed, by up to +0.7 / 0.5 / 0.3 m/s²; the cap shrinks in proportion as the model slows from -0.1 to -0.5 m/s² and as lateral accel goes 0.5 → 1.0 m/s², and the boost drops at once on model braking below -0.5 or a stop; a 3 s grace after an engage leads with a 1.2 m/s³ ramp from the truck's accel). Stops guardrail/barrier points on ramps and curves from braking the truck. The low-speed radar path (under 4 m/s) is untouched | `FM_RadarStationaryGuard` (Cruise > Smart Cruise Control) | `selfdrive/controls/radard.py`, `sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_radar.py` | off |
| Lead Detection Sensitivity: how sure the camera has to be (25–50%) that a car is ahead before it counts as a lead and is paired with the radar. Lower picks leads up sooner and farther away, so the lead-following planner gets radar distance and speed earlier; pair with Ignore Stationary Radar Ghosts | `FM_LeadDetectionThreshold` (Cruise > Smart Cruise Control) | `selfdrive/controls/radard.py`, `sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_radar.py` | 50 (openpilot's value) |
| Quiet road-edge block: small gray message, no sound (always on; `FM_QuietRoadEdgeBlock` is no longer read) | n/a | `selfdrived.py`, `sunnypilot/selfdrive/selfdrived/events.py`, `cereal/custom.capnp` | always |

| Turn Hold: keeps a model from letting go of a turn halfway (time stopped doesn't count toward its 8 s). Option Hold Path Source: when starting a turn from a stop or a crawl (under about 2 mph), also takes the turn from the planned path, never tighter than Turn Path Tightest Radius. Option Hold Adds Your Steering: steering into the turn never releases the hold and sets the curve to hold, up to full lock (not limited by the radius setting); steering against it still releases. Cancelling the blinker mid-hold fades the hold out over half a second instead of dropping it. Option Turn Nudge: at 2–8 mph, a deliberate nudge of the wheel into the signaled turn (0.8 Nm for 0.15 s) adds up to 0.01 1/m for 0.6 s when the nearby planned path already bends that way; steering against the turn cancels it | `FM_TurnHoldEnabled`, `FM_TurnHoldPathSource`, `FM_TurnHoldDriverAdds`, `FM_TurnNudgeEnabled` (Steering > FM Pilot: Turn Tweaks) | `fm_turn_assist.py` | off |
| Turn Lead: starts blinker turns on time when the model turns in late (from Turn Lead Min Speed, 2–7 mph, to about 16 mph), from the planned path's curve 1.3 s ahead, handing back as the truck catches up; never tighter than Turn Path Tightest Radius (shared with Hold Path Source). Can run with Turn Hold; one at a time recommended | `FM_TurnLeadEnabled`, `FM_TurnLeadMinSpeed`, `FM_TurnPathMinRadius` (Steering > FM Pilot: Turn Tweaks) | `fm_turn_assist.py` | off (7 mph; 12 m, 8–20) |
| Higher Torque (testing): up to **Max Torque** (405–580 in steps of 25, default 480) under about 20 mph (AP: 385), back to AP's curve by 38 mph, highway unchanged. **Panda Allows Higher Torque** and **Max Torque** (off-road only, apply next drive) set the panda's limit: the chosen level goes to the panda in the safety param (bits 2–5) and the panda enforces exactly it; **Higher Torque** picks Off / Headroom (more only where it used to max out) / Full (every low-speed request scaled by Max Torque / 385), live, blending over 1 s. With the panda setting off, every mode stays at 385. | Full Torque Past 90° (testing): with Panda Allows Higher Torque on, skips AP's 95% torque cap past 90° of steering wheel (`HIGH_ANGLE_CAP_FRAC`), so tight turns can use the full Max Torque. Car code only; the panda's limit is the same. Off-road only, applies next drive | `FM_TorqueFullCapHighAngle` (Steering, Torque Control) | `opendbc/car/rivian/carcontroller.py`, `opendbc/sunnypilot/car/interfaces.py` | off |
| Accel Boost bar: green bar in the comma 4 side panel (in place of the confidence ball) while Gas, Predictive or Set Speed boost is on; fills to that mode's maximum only while the boosted model target sets the speed | `FM_AccelBoostIndicator` (Cruise > Driving Personality Settings) | `selfdrive/ui/sunnypilot/mici/onroad/accel_boost_indicator.py`, `fm_boost_mode.py` | off |
| Torque readout: one line at the top center of the comma 4 road view, `sent / limit now / Max Torque ✓`. Sent is the raw `torqueOutputCan` (arrow = direction); limit now is the speed curve incl. the 95% past-90° cap; Max Torque is the panda limit this drive (385 without Panda Allows Higher Torque); ✓/✗ is the EPAS `ToiActive` bit (taking the overlay, not how much it applied). | Calmer Crawl-Speed Steering: below 5.6 mph, the model's lateral accel is turned into curvature with speed floored at 2.5 m/s instead of 1.0, so it asks for less (64% at 4.5 mph, 16% at 2.2 mph); calms crawl-speed jumps, may soften turns from a stop. All driving, not only blinker turns | `FM_LowSpeedCurvatureFloor` (Steering > FM Pilot: Turn Tweaks) | `sunnypilot/modeld_v2/modeld.py` | off |
| Lateral Smoothing: replaces the model's own `lat` value (its bundle override, `LAT_SMOOTH_SECONDS`) with 0, 0.1 or 0.2 s. That one number does two things: it low-passes the model's curvature (higher = calmer but later) and it is added to the lateral delay the model is given, so the plan is read further ahead (higher = starts steering earlier). The two pull opposite ways on turn-in. All driving; only modeld_v2 (not a Jetlink big model's own action path). Applies within about 3 s while driving. Off: the model's own value | `FM_LateralSmoothingEnabled`, `FM_LateralSmoothingSeconds` (Steering > FM Pilot: Turn Tweaks) | `sunnypilot/modeld_v2/modeld.py` | off (0.1 s) |

| Jetlink (branch `fm-dev-jet`): comma's big driving model runs on a Mac, Jetson, Linux PC or phone on the comma's USB-C port (USB 3 cable; comma powered from the harness, computer on its own power). ## Upstreaming to AdventurePilot

FM Pilot's history is personal: one early commit (`9a3d302db`) adds four features at once, and dual model mode was added
and later removed. To offer a feature upstream, start a fresh branch from AP's `dev` and carry over just that feature's
files and settings (listed in the table), with its tests, as one commit. Good candidates: Road Edge Turn Desire,
Turn Boost / Turn Hold, and the Quiet road-edge block.

## Findings

### Medium

1. **M-1 remains possible when NNLC becomes valid after the headroom ramp.** `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/nnlc/nnlc.py:65`, `openpilot/selfdrive/controls/controlsd.py:95`, `openpilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/latcontrol_torque.py:78`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/latcontrol_torque_v0.py:80`. With Max Torque 580, NNLC enabled, Lateral Jerk off and live torque parameters unavailable, let 120 disengaged frames elapse without a valid model. Then supply a valid model and engage at 5 m/s. In both real Rivian torque controllers, the synthetic probe used the configured NN model and the normal model-update call order. The PID positive limit remained **4.218182** for all ten active frames while the torque limit was **1.506494**. Binding the live PID fixes the original Lateral Jerk case. NNLC still changes its eligibility with `model_valid`, and neither a steady headroom cap nor unchanged overrides trigger a limit update. **Suggested fix.** Refresh PID limits when controller eligibility changes, before the first PID update in the new units. Cover the invalid-to-valid and valid-to-invalid transitions on v0 and v1 with live updates absent.

2. **M-2 suppresses new lane targets while all three modes can still apply existing correction.** `openpilot/sunnypilot/modeld_v2/modeld.py:561`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/modeld_v2/blendful.py:54`, `openpilot/selfdrive/controls/controlsd.py:149`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/lane_centering.py:128`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_lateral.py:106`, `openpilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/lane_guard.py:122`. On a quick blinker cancellation, the Blendful and AP Lane Centering release filters can still have a nonzero value. Lane Guard can have a live road-edge correction even with the blinker on. With carry confirmed, the first carry-frame additions in separate synthetic probes were **0.005424 1/m** for Blendful at 5 m/s, **0.000834 1/m** for AP Lane Centering at 6 m/s and **0.000731 1/m** for Lane Guard next to a detected edge at 8.5 m/s. Small-model and Jetlink actions share the Blendful callback that returns this addition. The shipped tests start lane correction at zero or first expose the close edge during carry. **Suggested fix.** Make carry suppress the applied lane contribution, including residual filters, while advancing or resetting the appropriate state and keeping Lane Guard's line cooldown armed. Test first and last carry frames with each correction already applied, then test normal resumption.

3. **M-2's Blendful gate can miss the start and keep suppressing Blendful after a carry has ended.** `openpilot/sunnypilot/modeld_v2/modeld.py:280`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/modeld_v2/modeld.py:462`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/modeld_v2/fm_turn_settle.py:454`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_lateral.py:76`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/controlsd_ext.py:138`. Modeld samples its own turn desire and the last asynchronously received carry status. If desire clears before the first carry status is observed, the gate admits Blendful. I supplied that ordering with trusted off-center lines and got a fresh nonzero Blendful contribution on the first model frame. The inverse ordering holds Blendful off for an extra frame at the end. There is also a persistent case. Disable the last enabled Turn Assist mode during an active carry and keep turning until the settings refresh. `FmLateral.update` stops stepping Turn Assist, while the publisher keeps serializing its old log state. After 210 more control updates, including straight frames, the real serializer still emitted `source=carry`, `sign=1`, and the Blendful predicate remained false. This extends the existing stale-log behavior to Blendful. **Suggested fix.** Clear the published carry and turn state when the last assist is disabled. For normal handoff, match each cleared desire to a fresh controls decision before letting Blendful add again. Test late start/end messages and disable during carry.

4. **Hold Adds Your Steering can rearm after the first 45° crossing.** `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_turn_assist.py:541`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_turn_assist.py:563`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_turn_assist.py:602`. Keep the blinker on, turn right through 46.10°, briefly countersteer to 41.90°, then press into the turn with actual curvature 0.16 1/m. With the hold still active, a synthetic run raised the peak from **0.10 to 0.16 1/m** and recorded **0.08 1/m** of driver addition. The cutoff and the signal-cancel pressure exemption both read the current signed heading integral, which can decrease. The model-exit eligibility check uses the same live threshold. **Suggested fix.** Remember the first crossing for Hold Adds Your Steering for the life of that turn. Apply it consistently to latching and pressure exemptions and make the intended model-exit behavior on recross explicit. Cover a 46° → 42° → press sequence and a signal cancel after the recross.

### Low

1. **AP Lane Centering can predict the wrong carry state on a settings-refresh frame.** `openpilot/selfdrive/controls/controlsd.py:149`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_lateral.py:64`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/fm_lateral.py:76`. Enable Finish Turn After Signal Cancels during a turn and cancel the signal on the next 1 s refresh tick. The prediction reads the old setting and the actual step refreshes it. In a synthetic drive, control frame 100 returned `predicted=False` and then logged `source=carry` for the same frame. Current randomized parity exercises stable settings and cannot detect this. **Suggested fix.** Use one parameter refresh and the same settings snapshot for the predictor and the actual step. Test enabling and disabling on the cancellation tick.

2. **Sunnylink's two Turn Hold descriptions still describe the old pressure behavior.** `openpilot/sunnypilot/sunnylink/settings_ui_src/pages/steering.yaml:660`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/sunnylink/settings_ui_src/pages/steering.yaml:669`. At 53°, with Hold Adds Your Steering and Hold Follows the Model's Exit enabled, the model can start the fade while the driver presses into the turn. The generated UI still says steering into the turn never releases Hold and that the fade waits for the driver to ease off. The folded defaults and handoff describe the new behavior. **Suggested fix.** Update both descriptions in YAML and regenerate `settings_ui.json`.

3. **The M-1 override test passes if the override-specific restoration is removed.** `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/tests/test_fm_headroom_limits.py:81`, `openpilot/sunnypilot/selfdrive/controls/lib/latcontrol_torque_ext_override.py:23`. The test applies the override on the first update at 5 m/s. That frame also increments headroom and invokes a second limit restore. Suppressing the first restore in memory left the assertion passing on both controllers during ramp-up. After 300 steps, suppressing the restore on the next polling frame left the limit at **4.218182** with a steady cap of **1.506494**. **Suggested fix.** Make the test change override values on a polling frame after headroom stabilizes and assert the override took effect alongside both torque-unit limits.

## Behavior scope

The 45° path passes the monotonic-turn tests. An input added before 45° remains in the peak as its normal floor decays. On a continued turn at 53°, new same-direction pressure adds nothing and the model-exit fade can complete. Sustained pressure can still invoke normal driver release. The recross finding limits that guarantee.

The first-parent diffs touch no panda firmware, panda safety rules, Rivian carcontroller, driver-monitoring code or selfdrived escalation logic. L-1 changes the configured safety level for a corrupt non-finite Max Torque input to stock. The valid-input limit table, encoded levels and enforcement are unchanged. M-1 intentionally changes PID request limits in the failing conditions. The changed files support the described scope, subject to the findings above. Independent synthetic comparisons with the immediate pre-PR revisions matched **28,800 frames each**, including **1,883 carry frames** per run, for #27's Turn Assist refactor and #26 with driver-adds off and Settle idle.
